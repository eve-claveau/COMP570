1. What is refactoring? Give 3 examples
Refactoring is reorganizing the code without changing its functionality. For example, changing a variable name, altering the contract of a function, changing the data type used in a particular spot...

2. In a data science project, why does code naturally go through "phases" of messiness?
When coding, you break the task into small tasks. You get those parts working, and meanwhile, you are going through a phase of messiness. Once you figure out every part, you then need to connect the parts, meanwhile, you are going through another phase of messiness.


3. What are three techniques for creating more modular code?
If you are doing something multiple times, it should be a function. If your function is more than 50 lines long, you should break it into more functions. Your mean function should read like a recipe.
